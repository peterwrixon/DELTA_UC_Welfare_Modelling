## 1. Quality Assurance & Reproducible Analytical Pipeline (RAP) Architecture

This repository implements a production-grade **Reproducible Analytical Pipeline (RAP)** engineered in compliance with:
1. **UK Government Analysis Function — Quality Assurance of Code for Analysis and Research ("The Duck Book", Version 2026.1):** Enforces modular functional programming, deterministic pseudo-random seeding, inline dependency isolation, and automated unit testing.
2. **HM Treasury — The Aqua Book: Guidance on Producing Quality Analysis for Government:** Separates immutable input parameters from analytical logic and subjects core assumptions to multi-channel sensitivity perturbations.
3. **ONS & Eurostat — European System of Accounts 2010 (ESA 2010) & Manual on Government Deficit and Debt (MGDD Part II, Chapter 2.4):** Verifies the >50% present-value (PV) recoverability threshold for balance-sheet Financial Transactions (F.4 in Capital AME) and reconciles Public Sector Current Budget Deficit (PSCB), Public Sector Net Investment (PSNI), and Public Sector Net Borrowing (PSNB).

### Dependencies & Execution
The simulation requires **Python 3.10 or later**, **NumPy 1.24.0 or later**, and **pandas 2.0.0 or later**. These requirements are declared in the script's **PEP 723 inline metadata**; no external CSV or spreadsheet files are required.

With `uv`, the inline metadata is used to install the dependencies automatically:

- **Using `uv` (recommended):**
  `uv run uc_epc_welfare_model.py > uc_welfare_engine_outputs.md`

With a standard Python installation, install the packages first, then run the script:

- **Using standard Python 3.10+:**
  `python -m pip install "numpy>=1.24.0" "pandas>=2.0.0" && python uc_epc_welfare_model.py > uc_welfare_engine_outputs.md`

On every execution, `main()` runs the embedded `TestWelfareReformRAP` `unittest.TestCase` suite to `stderr` before printing any output tables. If any physical, actuarial, or National Accounts identity fails, execution halts immediately with exit code `1`.

## 3. Module-by-Module Mathematical & Algorithmic Specification

### Module 1: `calculate_equivalised_caseload_and_comp1(cfg)`
Stratifies the 7,200,000 Universal Credit households across six demographic archetypes using the **Modified OECD Equivalence Scale**:

> **Equivalence factor (Eₕ)** = 1.0 + 0.5 × (adults − 1) + 0.3 × (children under 14) + 0.5 × (children aged 14+)

Summing Eₕ × Householdsₕ yields **9,914,400 adult-equivalent units** (9.9m rounded) and a caseload-weighted mean equivalence factor of Ēₕ = 1.377 (1.4 rounded). Multiplying the single-adult Component 1 baseline (£60.00/wk = £3,120.00/yr) by Eₕ across all archetypes yields a caseload mean entitlement of **£82.62/wk (£4,296/yr; £83/wk and £4,300/yr to 2 s.f.)** and an aggregate outlay of **£30.93bn/yr (£30.9bn/yr)**.

### Module 2: `calculate_epc_utilities_matrix(cfg, ...)`
Computes the physical energy (in kWh/yr) and net financial outlay required to maintain a clinically safe **19°C indoor temperature** across EPC Bands A to G:
1. **Useful Space & Water Heating Demand:** For each EPC band (A–G), useful heating demand is floor area (66.7282 m²) × SAP rating for the band × occupancy factor (0.92).
2. **Fuel Vector Disaggregation:**
   - **Dual-Fuel Dwellings (82% of UC stock):** Divides useful heat by condensing gas boiler seasonal efficiency (η = 0.86) at the Ofgem gas unit rate (£0.0620/kWh) plus gas standing charge (£108.33/yr), alongside daytime baseload electricity (2,850 kWh/yr) at £0.2450/kWh plus electricity standing charge (£202.30/yr).
   - **Electricity-Only Dwellings (18% of UC stock):** Divides useful heat by the blended direct-electric/heat-pump Coefficient of Performance (COP = 1.45) and adds baseload electricity (2,850 kWh/yr) at £0.2450/kWh plus £202.30/yr standing charge.
3. **CCS Bulk Procurement & Total Utility Grant:** Applies the 10.5% Crown Commercial Service (CCS) B2G bulk energy multiplier (`0.895`, stripping out retail supplier bad-debt levies, customer acquisition/switching costs, and excess retail EBIT margins) and adds Ofwat PR24 water (£490.00/yr) and Ofcom social FTTP broadband + 50GB 5G mobile (£312.00/yr). Across the stock distribution, this yields **8,800 kWh/yr** of delivered heating energy, **11,650 kWh/yr (12,000 kWh rounded)** total delivered energy, and a caseload-weighted net utility grant of **£2,342.00/yr (£2,300/yr to 2 s.f. = £16.86bn/yr [£16.9bn/yr])**.
4. **PRS Landlord Thermal Penalty Clawback:** For Private Rented Sector dwellings rated EPC Band D to G, excess thermal cost above the EPC Band C benchmark (£2,193.55/yr [£2,200/yr rounded])—equal to **£230/yr in Band D, £510/yr in Band E, £850/yr in Band F, and £1,200/yr in Band G (£180/yr stock-weighted mean)**—is deducted at source from the landlord's monthly Component 5 LHA payment.

### Module 3: `simulate_icr_loans_monte_carlo(cfg, re_emp_prob_shift)`
Executes a **50,000-draw Monte Carlo simulation** seeded deterministically at `rng_seed = 202601` using NumPy's `PCG64` generator (`np.random.default_rng(202601)`):
- **Interest Rate Parity (Zero Real Interest Subsidy):** Both ICR facilities accrue interest strictly at the **3.5% nominal HM Treasury Green Book Social Time Preference Rate (STPR)**, ensuring that the discounted present value of every £1.00 of future principal and interest repaid equals £1.00.
- **Section 4 Automated 3-Month Mortgage Arrears ICR Loan:** Simulates 35,000 owner-occupier borrowers/yr (£5,500 mean principal = £192.50m/yr CAME), combining 2.5% PAYE deductions above £24,500/yr with the HM Land Registry Statutory Subordinated Second Charge at property sale or probate. Achieves a **94.1% (94%) Expected PV Recovery Rate** and a **5.9%–6.1% Cohort RAB Charge (£11.7m/yr RDEL)**.
- **Component 6 Essential Goods ICR Micro-Loan:** Simulates 377,280 working-age borrowers/yr (£310 wholesale A-rated appliance principal = £116.96m/yr CAME) repaying at 3.0% of gross earnings above £24,500/yr over a 20-year working-life horizon. Achieves a **56.4% (56%) Expected PV Recovery Rate** and a **43.6% (44%) Cohort RAB Charge (£51.0m/yr RDEL)**. Both facilities strictly exceed the **>50% PV recoverability threshold** required under ESA 2010 and Eurostat/ONS MGDD Part II Chapter 2.4 for F.4 Financial Transaction classification.

### Module 4: `compute_multi_horizon_scorecard(cfg, comp1_res, comp2_res)`
Reconciles expenditure, dynamic offsets, and tax yields across Years 1, 5, 10, 15, 20, and 25 in constant 2026 £bn/yr:
1. **Gross Marginal Outlay:** Starts at **+£36.92bn/yr (+£36.9bn)** in Year 1 (+£35.98bn core uplift across Components 1–7 + £0.50bn DHSC prescription/dental/optical and Sure Start passporting + £0.44bn Phase 1/2 Study Hub CDEL and flex-lease mobilisation). From Year 5 onwards, reflects the **-£0.80bn/yr** statutory baseline progression of the *Universal Credit (Removal of Two Child Limit) Act 2026* and declining Hub session/lease outlays as adult unemployment falls (**+£35.50bn in Year 5; +£35.11bn by Year 20–25**).
2. **Macroeconomic & Public-Service Offsets (-£9.92bn Y1 -> -£24.42bn Y25):**
   - *Line 2 (MPC & Indirect Tax Clawbacks):* In Year 1, the k = 1.28 open-economy expenditure multiplier (MPC = 0.96, t = 0.20, m = 0.21) returns **-£7.21bn/yr** across direct VAT/excise (-£3.40bn), public Hub staff PAYE/NICs (-£0.12bn), and induced supply-chain payroll/corporate taxes (-£3.69bn). By Year 5, in accordance with OBR monetary policy neutrality rules, the cyclical demand multiplier (-£3.69bn) decays to £0, leaving the permanent **-£3.52bn/yr (-£3.5bn)** structural indirect tax and Hub staff PAYE composition return.
   - *Lines 3 & 4 (Adult Re-Employment PAYE/NICs & DWP Caseload/Debt Exits):* Tracks the S-curve transition of **400,000 unemployed adult claimants** into work (50k Y1 -> 200k Y5 -> 310k Y10 -> 380k Y15 -> 400k Y20–25), generating **-£0.56bn/yr in Year 1 rising to -£4.45bn/yr (-£4.5bn) in Years 20–25**.
   - *Lines 5, 6 & 7a (Core Preventative NHS, HMCTS & Local Authority TA/Care Savings):* Sums adult mental health, 19°C cold-home respiratory/cardiovascular prevention, and NHS Pharmacy First substitution of 15m GP/A&E visits (-£1.35bn Y1 to -£4.90bn Y25), HMCTS civil possession/eviction and criminal justice savings (-£0.23bn Y1 to -£1.00bn Y15–25), and Local Authority nightly B&B Temporary Accommodation and acute Section 17/47 placement reductions (-£0.46bn Y1 to -£3.75bn Y15–25).
   - *Line 7b (Generational Child Poverty Eradication Dividend):* Scored strictly on the marginal cohort of children lifted out of poverty above the April/September 2026 statutory baseline, rising from **-£0.11bn in Year 1 and -£0.60bn in Year 5 to -£6.80bn/yr in Year 25** (preventing 135,000 youth NEETs).
3. **ESA 2010 National Accounts & Post-Tax Surplus:** Deducts net capital investment (`PSNI = £0.31bn` in Year 1; `£0.00bn` from Year 5) and net CAME F.4 loan / capital-account adjustments (`£0.57bn` in Year 1; `£0.69bn` in Years 5–25) to derive **Pre-Tax PSCB (+£26.12bn Y1 -> +£24.52bn Y5 -> +£10.00bn Y25)**. Netting the six-instrument Capital-Tax Portfolio (**-£26.50bn/yr in Year 1 rising to -£32.40bn/yr from Year 5** as CGT and LLP realisations mature) yields a **Post-Tax Current Budget Surplus (Net PSCB) of -£0.38bn (-£0.4bn) in Year 1, -£7.88bn (-£7.9bn) in Year 5, -£12.71bn (-£12.7bn) in Year 10, -£17.49bn (-£17.5bn) in Year 15, -£20.06bn (-£20.1bn) in Year 20, and -£22.40bn (-£22.4bn) in Year 25**.

## 4. Automated Unit Test Verification Suite (`TestWelfareReformRAP`)

| Unit Test Method | Verification Invariant Enforced |
|---|---|
| `test_01_oecd_equivalence_and_caseload_conservation` | Verifies archetype shares sum to `1.000000000`, total households equal `7,200,000`, and caseload-weighted OECD equivalence equals `1.377`. |
| `test_02_epc_thermal_monotonicity_and_landlord_penalty` | Verifies delivered 19°C kWh and net utility costs increase strictly monotonically across EPC Bands A to G, caseload heating equals `8,800 kWh/yr`, net utility grant equals `£2,342/yr`, and landlord penalties are `£0` in Bands A–C and `> £0` in Bands D–G. |
| `test_03_esa2010_mgdd_50pct_icr_recoverability` | Verifies Mortgage ICR PV recovery exceeds `90%` (`RAB < 10%`) and Component 6 Appliance ICR PV recovery exceeds the statutory `50%` ESA 2010 / MGDD F.4 threshold (`RAB < 50%`). |
| `test_04_multi_horizon_scorecard_accounting_identities` | Verifies across all six horizons (Years 1 to 25) that `Pre-Tax PSCB + PSNI == Pre-Tax PSNB`, `Pre-Tax PSCB + Tax Yield == Post-Tax PSCB`, and `Post-Tax PSCB < 0.0` (strict Current Budget surplus in every period). |
| `test_05_precision_and_neg_formatting_guardrails` | Verifies `fmt_bn()` enforces the `£0.1bn` macro precision cap and returns `'neg'` for `|val| < £0.05bn`, and `fmt_sf()` rounds micro parameters strictly to 2 significant figures. |
